# Store listing — English

## Name (75 max)

What about the dollar? - Live US dollar exchange rates in Argentina

## Summary (132 max)

Official, blue, MEP, CCL and crypto dollar rates on your toolbar. Daily change, converter and alerts when it crosses your price.

## Description

What about the dollar? puts the Argentine dollar exchange rates right on your
browser toolbar. One number, always current, without opening another tab.

━━━ THE RATES ━━━

• Official
• Blue (parallel)
• MEP (stock exchange)
• CCL (contado con liquidación)
• Wholesale
• Crypto
• Card

Optionally the euro, Brazilian real, Chilean peso and Uruguayan peso too.

━━━ WHAT YOU CAN DO ━━━

▸ SEE THE PRICE WITHOUT OPENING ANYTHING
Pick the rate you care about and its value sits on the toolbar icon. Hover it
for the detail: buying price, selling price and when it was last updated.

▸ SEE HOW MUCH IT MOVED TODAY
Every rate shows its change against yesterday's close, with a sparkline of
the last thirty closes.

▸ CONVERT IN ONE STEP
Type an amount and it converts pesos to dollars, or the other way round, at
the rate you choose.

▸ CONVERT FROM ANY PAGE
Select a number anywhere, right-click, and it tells you what it is worth —
in both directions.

▸ GET A HEADS-UP
Set a value and get a notification when the selling price crosses it. Once
per crossing, no spam.

▸ COPY A VALUE
Click any price and it goes to your clipboard.

━━━ ALSO ━━━

• Light and dark themes, or whatever the browser is using.
• Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+D.
• Your settings follow you to every machine signed into your Chrome account.
• Available in Spanish and English.

━━━ PRIVACY ━━━

No personal data is collected. No analytics, no trackers, nothing loaded from
third parties.

The only requests it makes are to dolarapi.com for the rates and
argentinadatos.com for the history. Both are read-only and carry no
information about you.

When you use the right-click converter, the selected number is used to work
out the result and nothing else: it is never stored or sent anywhere.

The source code is open and can be reviewed:
https://github.com/maurocartagironi/chrome-extension-Y-el-dolar-che
